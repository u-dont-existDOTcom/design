# Anti-Slop Gate

The gate detects generic generated-design defaults without replacing one template with another.

## Critical integrity failures

Fabricated proof, silent argument compression, fluent unsupported claims, copied design DNA presented as original, and destructive replacement outside scope block delivery.

## Structural signals

Investigate repeated hero/equal-card/CTA/footer rhythms, genre-blind navigation, equal-card grids for unequal content, nested containment, identical section scaffolds, repeated macrostructure across unrelated products, and page apparatus applied to components.

## Visual signals

Investigate fashionable effects without product roles, default-font sameness, mixed icon voices, motion everywhere, trust-weakening imagery, color-only states, and uniform cards where cards have no semantic role.

## Verbal signals

Investigate generic benefit language replacing owner claims, unsupported trust phrases, invented social proof, template labels, repeated style tics, and polished errors without recovery guidance.

## Corrective method

Name the signal, classify intentional/accidental/unclear, identify its purpose, remove it if purposeless, replace it from the brief, and rerun lower-layer/accessibility checks.

A coherent brief may legitimately use centered composition, maximalism, brutalism, glass, gradients, unusual type, dense controls, or repeated structures. Document and verify the purpose. The gate cannot normalize a clear brief into agent taste.
